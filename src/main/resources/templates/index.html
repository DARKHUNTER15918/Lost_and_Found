How Campus Lost & Found Works

Simple 4-step process for fast and transparent recovery

1. Report Item

Fill out a simple form with item details, location, date, and upload a picture.

2. Search & Match

Browse listings using filters by category, location, and keywords across campus.

3. Submit Claim

Provide proof of ownership (serial number, distinctive marks, password hint, etc.).

4. Verify & Handover

Admin reviews the claim, verifies proof, and coordinates secure handover.
